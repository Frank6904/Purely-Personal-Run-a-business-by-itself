The Three Roads

Eighteen months from today, your client is standing on one of three roads. This model makes the choice visible without pressure: you cannot guarantee the outcome, but you can guarantee the pathway.

Your three roads, drawing themselveslive
1
Today, without judgementthe dot
Numbers where possible: hours worked, revenue, leads per month, energy level. No shame in the dot: "there is nothing wrong with today."
Before drawing any roads, rule a line on the past. Write your version of: "Let's put a peg in the sand today. Everything before today doesn't matter anymore. And it wasn't entirely your fault: the system was never set up to make this easy for you." Shame looks backward; this model needs them looking forward.
2
The green road: actthe pathway
The road where they start now and stay on the path. Milestones, not miracles: what is true at 6, 12 and 18 months if they simply keep walking? Think of each milestone as a gate you walk through and shut behind you. That is what a plan is.
3
The grey road: driftthe honest middle
Not a disaster. Just today, repeated 540 times, with a little more fatigue. The grey road is the most crowded one, and the most expensive, because it feels free.
4
The red road: erodewhat decays
Markets move. Which client, skill, or position quietly erodes? Who is learning faster? Be factual, not frightening: the model does the work, you stay kind.
5
The gap and the guaranteethe close
One line about the space between green and red at 18 months. It goes above the drawing. Example: "the gap is not talent, it is a decision, compounding."
You cannot guarantee the outcome. What CAN you guarantee about the pathway? The steps, the support, the checkpoints. And remember the line that closes this model: "Right now, in this moment, you are as close to the green road as you will ever be." Nobody falls off the green road; they behave their way off it.
