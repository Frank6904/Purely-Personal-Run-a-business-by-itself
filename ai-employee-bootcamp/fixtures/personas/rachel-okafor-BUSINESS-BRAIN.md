## 1 · IDENTITY
- **Name:** Rachel Okafor
- **Business:** Ship & Lead
- **Positioning:** The leadership coach who turns senior engineers into managers their teams don't quit on, inside the first 90 days.
- **Unique angle:** 12 years as an engineer and EM at two unicorns. Got demoted from her first management role, rebuilt, then ran a 40-person org. Coaches from the postmortem, not the podium.
- **Never sound like:** HR-speak, LinkedIn hustle accounts, therapy-adjacent life coaches.

## 2 · ICP
- **Who exactly:** Newly promoted engineering managers at Series B to D startups, managing 4 to 10 engineers, promoted within the last 12 months, still writing code they shouldn't be.
- **Their pain, their words:** "I'm in meetings all day and still expected to ship." / "My best engineer resigned and I didn't see it coming." / "Nobody told me what this job actually is." / "Am I even allowed to stop coding?"
- **What they want most:** A team that ships without them in every thread, and a skip-level who notices.
- **Already tried:** A management book their VP recommended. A Udemy course. Copying their old manager, who was also winging it.
- **Where they hang out:** LinkedIn, Rands Leadership Slack, engineering leadership newsletters.
- **Sophistication:** High on systems thinking, low on people vocabulary. They respect precision and hate fluff.

## 3 · OFFER
- **Offer name:** The First 90 Group
- **Promise:** In 12 weeks, run a team that ships without you in every thread, with a change your skip-level can name.
- **Mechanism:** Weekly 90-minute cohort call, one real-team experiment per week, async code-review-style feedback on your actual manager artifacts (1:1 notes, team docs, calendars).
- **Price and terms:** $2,400 per seat, max 12 seats per cohort.
- **Guarantee:** Finish all 12 weeks and if your skip-level can't name one visible change in how your team runs, full refund.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a 15-minute fit call.

## 5 · VOICE DNA
- **10-second summary:** Warm, blunt, engineer-brained. Talks about management the way good engineers talk about incidents: blameless, specific, slightly funny.
- **Rhythm:** Short sentences. One sharp question per piece. Analogies from code review and on-call, never from sports.
- **Signature phrases:** "Your calendar is your architecture." / "Attrition is a lagging indicator of you." / "Stop debugging people in public."
- **Openers:** "Your best engineer just went quiet in standup." / "Nobody gets promoted into management. They get thrown."
- **Banned words (hers):** rockstar, 10x, superpower, servant-leader, journey
- **Energy:** 7/10. Direct, a little wry, never hype.
- **Sign-off:** "Ship people, not code. Rachel"
- **Writing samples:** "You didn't lose an engineer. You lost the six weeks of signals before the resignation. The exit interview is just the stack trace." / "Your 1:1 is not a status meeting. If it can be a Slack message, it was never a 1:1."
